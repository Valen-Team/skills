# Labeling rules (Reelbank clip tags)

Tag what the clip shows for most of its length.

## setting
1. `kitchen`: a kitchen or a cooking surface with a stove, hob or pot in view.
2. `indoor`: any other room or building interior, including gyms, offices and stages.
3. `outdoor`: outside, including stadiums, streets, gardens, beaches and aerial views.
4. `screen`: animation, cartoons or computer graphics rather than filmed footage.

## activity
5. `cooking`: preparing food on screen (chopping, stirring, frying, pouring into a pan or pot), wherever it happens.
6. `sport_or_exercise`: playing a sport, training or racing, including motorsport and arm wrestling.
7. `talking`: one or more people speaking to the camera or to each other is the main thing happening.
8. `other`: everything else: daily chores, playing, performing music, dancing, walking, scenery, animals, crafts.

## people
9. Count real people visible in the clip: `none`, `one` or `several` (two or more at the same time).
10. Hands or legs of one person count as `one`. Cartoon or animated characters count as `none`.
